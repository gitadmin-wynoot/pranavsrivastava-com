## Why this track exists

Most AI content online skips the foundations. You get prompting tutorials without understanding why transformers work. You get code without understanding what loss functions actually mean. You get results without understanding when they will fail.

This track closes that gap. It covers the mathematical and conceptual layer that makes everything else — LLMs, agents, vector search, computer vision — actually understandable rather than just usable.

## What you will learn

- The mathematics AI is built on: linear algebra, probability, statistics, calculus
- Supervised and unsupervised machine learning — with real intuition for what is happening inside each algorithm
- Deep learning architectures from first principles — CNNs, RNNs, transformers
- Natural Language Processing before and after LLMs
- Computer vision: feature detection, CNNs, tracking, pose estimation
- Knowledge representation and knowledge graphs
- Metaheuristic optimisation — genetic algorithms, simulated annealing, swarm intelligence

## Who this is for

- Developers who have been using AI tools and want to understand what is actually happening
- Engineers preparing for roles in applied ML or AI systems
- Learners who want the academic rigour behind the buzzwords

You do not need a maths degree. You need patience with equations and a desire to understand things properly.

## What this unlocks

Completing this track gives you the foundations to move into:
- **Applied AI** — building LLM systems, agents, and semantic search
- **Data Engineering** — feature engineering and ML pipelines make much more sense with this background

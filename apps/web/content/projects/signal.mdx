## Think alongside the machine

Three rescue missions turn classical AI search into a visual game. Plot a human route, step through the AI's reasoning, predict its next decision, and compare the result against an independent shortest-path calculation.

Switch between A*, Dijkstra, and weighted A*. Explore Manhattan distance, consistency, the A* optimality theorem, and a concrete counterexample to an overconfident search. A sandbox lets you test your own maps.

Runs entirely in the browser, with no model API or account required.

## Question the signal

The second chapter puts visitors in charge of a rescue under uncertainty. Three scenarios teach Bayesian updates and base rates, independence and duplicate evidence, expected information gain, and Bellman’s principle of optimality.

Predict the posterior before the AI reveals its answer. Inspect the arithmetic, replay a packet to expose double-counting, and decide whether the next scan is worth its cost. A finite-horizon planner evaluates all possible scan outcomes; a separate information-gain view makes the difference between curiosity and expected reward visible.

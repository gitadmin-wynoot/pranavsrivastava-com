## What you will learn

Deep learning is the engine of modern AI. This course builds from a single neuron to the transformer architecture — understanding each step rather than treating it as a black box.

### Neural network foundations
- The perceptron and why single-layer networks fail
- Multilayer perceptrons and universal approximation
- Activation functions: sigmoid, ReLU, GELU — and why the choice matters
- Backpropagation: the algorithm, the chain rule, the implementation

### Convolutional neural networks
- Why convolution works for images — translation invariance
- Architecture patterns: VGG, ResNet, EfficientNet
- Transfer learning: using pre-trained models on new problems
- Data augmentation and its effect on generalisation

*This section is informed by hands-on project work including flower classification with CNNs and transfer learning.*

### Sequence models
- Recurrent neural networks and vanishing gradients
- LSTMs and GRUs — gating mechanisms
- Why sequence models matter for time-series and NLP

### Attention and transformers
- The attention mechanism — what it computes and why
- Self-attention, multi-head attention, positional encoding
- The transformer architecture — encoder, decoder, encoder-decoder
- Why transformers replaced RNNs

### Training in practice
- Batch normalisation and layer normalisation
- Learning rate schedules and warm-up
- Gradient clipping
- Mixed precision training

## Course outline

1. From perceptron to multilayer networks
2. Backpropagation — derivation and intuition
3. Activation functions and vanishing gradients
4. CNNs: convolution, pooling, receptive fields
5. CNN architectures and reading ResNets
6. Transfer learning in practice
7. Sequence modelling: RNNs, LSTMs, GRUs
8. The attention mechanism
9. Transformers: architecture and training
10. Training tricks: normalisation, schedules, precision
11. Mini-project: build and train a classifier end to end

## What you will learn

NLP covers the gap between raw text and machine understanding. This course covers the full arc — from classical text processing to the transformer-based systems that power modern LLMs.

### Classical NLP
- Text preprocessing: tokenisation, stemming, lemmatisation
- Bag-of-words and TF-IDF representations
- N-gram models and statistical language modelling
- Named entity recognition (NER) and part-of-speech tagging

### Word embeddings
- Word2Vec — skip-gram and CBOW objectives
- GloVe and FastText
- Why distributed representations changed NLP

### Neural NLP
- Sequence-to-sequence models for translation and summarisation
- Attention mechanisms applied to NLP
- BERT and the masked language model objective
- Fine-tuning pre-trained models on classification tasks

*This section draws from hands-on work building personality-based chatbots with intent classification and speech-to-text context extraction.*

### Modern LLMs (bridge to Applied AI track)
- GPT-style autoregressive language modelling
- Prompt engineering as a new programming paradigm
- What LLMs can and cannot do
- Hallucination, calibration, and factual grounding

## Course outline

1. Text as data: preprocessing and representation
2. Bag-of-words to TF-IDF
3. Word embeddings: Word2Vec and GloVe
4. Sequence models for NLP
5. Attention applied to text
6. BERT and contextual representations
7. Intent classification and NER in practice
8. Fine-tuning language models
9. Bridge to LLMs: autoregression and prompting

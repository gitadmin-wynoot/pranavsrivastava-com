## What you will learn

Not all optimisation problems have gradients. When the search space is discrete, combinatorial, or noisy — genetic algorithms, simulated annealing, and particle swarm optimisation become the right tools. This course covers why, when, and how to use them.

### Why metaheuristics
- The landscape of optimisation problems
- When gradient-based methods fail: discrete search, multimodal functions, non-differentiable objectives
- No Free Lunch theorem: why no single algorithm dominates all problems
- NP-hard problems and approximation

### Evolutionary algorithms
- Genetic algorithms: representation, selection, crossover, mutation
- Fitness landscapes and premature convergence
- Genetic programming
- Evolution strategies and CMA-ES

*This is backed by direct thesis research — solving the Travelling Salesman Problem with genetic algorithms including custom crossover operators for permutation encodings.*

### Swarm intelligence
- Particle Swarm Optimisation: velocity, inertia, social and cognitive components
- Ant Colony Optimisation for graph problems
- Bee algorithms

### Simulated annealing and local search
- Hill climbing and its limitations
- Simulated annealing: Metropolis criterion, cooling schedules
- Tabu search

### Modern applications in AI
- Neural Architecture Search (NAS)
- Hyperparameter optimisation: Bayesian vs. evolutionary approaches
- Prompt optimisation as a search problem
- Differentiable + evolutionary hybrid methods

## Course outline

1. The optimisation problem landscape
2. Genetic algorithms from scratch
3. Crossover and mutation strategies for combinatorial problems
4. Solving TSP: a worked example
5. Evolution strategies and CMA-ES
6. Particle Swarm Optimisation
7. Simulated annealing and tabu search
8. Metaheuristics in modern AI: NAS, HPO, prompt optimisation

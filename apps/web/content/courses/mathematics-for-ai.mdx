## Why this course exists

You can use an AI API without understanding the maths. But you cannot build reliable AI systems, debug model failures, or evaluate research papers without it.

This course builds the mathematical intuition that makes everything else click.

## What you will learn

### Linear algebra
- Vectors, matrices, and tensors — what they actually are
- Matrix multiplication as transformation
- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors — and why they matter for PCA and attention
- Singular Value Decomposition

### Probability and statistics
- Probability distributions — Gaussian, Bernoulli, categorical
- Bayes' theorem and Bayesian thinking
- Maximum likelihood estimation
- Information theory: entropy, KL divergence, cross-entropy

### Calculus for optimisation
- Derivatives and gradients — the intuition, not just the mechanics
- The chain rule — why it is the foundation of backpropagation
- Gradient descent: batch, stochastic, mini-batch
- Common optimisers: SGD, Adam, AdamW

### Why this is in every AI system
- Loss functions are minimised with calculus
- Weights are matrices (linear algebra)
- Outputs are probability distributions (statistics)

## Course outline

1. Vectors and geometric intuition
2. Matrix operations and transformations
3. Eigendecomposition and SVD
4. Probability fundamentals
5. Common distributions in ML
6. Bayes' theorem in practice
7. Information theory: entropy and KL divergence
8. Derivatives and gradient descent
9. The chain rule and backpropagation
10. Optimisers: from SGD to Adam

There has never been a faster time to build. You can have a working thing by lunch. The tools are astonishing and the pressure is relentless: ship, post, move, before the window closes and the next thing arrives. I feel it too. Some days the speed is a gift. Some days it feels like being hurried past everything worth stopping for.

I want to make a quiet case for the opposite. Not slowness for its own sake — I am not romantic about that. A case for building *some* things slowly, on purpose, because slow is the only way they come out well.

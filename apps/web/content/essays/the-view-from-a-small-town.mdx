Most of the future, as far as AI is concerned, gets imagined in a small number of rooms in a small number of cities. Brilliant rooms, full of brilliant people. But the problems that fill those rooms are, naturally, the problems those people have. And the world is mostly made of other people, with other problems, who are not in the room.

I know this because I started a long way from any of those rooms.

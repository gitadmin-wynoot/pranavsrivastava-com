The demo always works.

That is the most dangerous sentence in AI right now. You wire up a model, feed it
a clean example, and it does something that looks like magic. Everyone in the room
nods. The proof of concept is "done."

Then it meets production — real users, real volume, real money on the line — and
the magic quietly falls apart. I have watched this happen enough times, across
enough industries, to believe the gap between *demo* and *dependable* is the real
work. The model was never the hard part.
